# Suffering

See ethics post.

One goal that a lot of people might be willing to  get behind is the reduction of suffering.
There a lot of different ways people categorize suffering:

- Human vs animal
- Personal vs others
- suffering we witness vs that which we don't
- Great vs minuscule
- short term vs long term

What if there was a database for tracking all kinds of suffering.
Each record would specify a time-span such as a few seconds of suffering to ongoing for years.
There'd be a way of ranking the suffering: maybe people could vote what's worse than what.
Ranking may be easier and less subjective than scoring, although weighting an individual's vote allocation across a variety of areas might allow useful nuance.
There'd need to be consideration of chain reactions: suffering can lead to more suffering, just as trauma or revenge.

Anyway, it wouldn't be a perfect model of suffering, but it would be better than the Wild West we have now where our feeling about what's worst depends largely on what we've most recently been confronted with.
A model, even a half-decent one, allows constructive discussion.
We could begin to quantify the effects of action,  such as charity and government policy.
Numbers based on data, especially if the numbers are detailed and always fresh, can give people an excuse to make decisions and take action, which is sometimes all they are waiting for.
